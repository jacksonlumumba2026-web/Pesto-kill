Nairobi Pest Control: What the Assessment Covers

Property type and affected rooms or zones are identified so the treatment is matched to the actual site.

The technician considers pest activity, harbourages, access points, food or water sources and other conditions that may be contributing to recurrence.

Detailed service guide

Nairobi pest control built around the property

Customers need more than a generic spray. A useful service starts with understanding the property, the pest activity, the affected areas and the conditions that may allow the problem to return.

For homes and apartments

We can help with bedbugs, cockroaches, ants, rodents, mosquitoes, flies, termites and other household pest concerns. The service scope should match the rooms affected and the treatment method required.

For landlords and property managers

Where several units or common areas are involved, Pestokil can help define the affected zones, preparation requirements and follow-up needs so the work is easier to coordinate.

For businesses

Offices, restaurants, hotels, warehouses and other premises may require a documented service scope, operating-hour coordination and practical prevention measures alongside treatment.
