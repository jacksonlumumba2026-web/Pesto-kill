# Pestokil SEO & Local Authority Setup

## Google Business Profile
- Verify ownership and keep business name, category, address/service area, phone and hours accurate.
- Add the services listed on the website with accurate descriptions.
- Add real team/work photos regularly.
- Request genuine reviews after completed jobs; never offer incentives for reviews.
- Reply to reviews professionally.
- Add the website and any eligible customer-action links.
- Review GBP Performance monthly: searches, views, calls, website actions and direction requests.

## Google Search Console
- Add and verify `https://pestokil.co.ke/`.
- Submit `https://pestokil.co.ke/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect the homepage, Nairobi service page and key service/location pages after deployment.
- Monitor indexing, Core Web Vitals and search queries.

## Reviews
- Publish only genuine customer reviews with permission/source.
- Do not create or edit customer statements to make them look better.

## Credentials
- Add licence/certificate scans only after checking validity and permission to publish.
- Record document name, issuing body and validity where appropriate.

## Case studies
- Add only genuine Pestokil jobs.
- Use customer-approved photos and anonymised property details.
- Record problem, inspection, treatment, preparation, follow-up and outcome.

## Local citations & backlinks
- Keep name/address/phone consistent.
- Prioritise relevant Kenyan business directories, property-management associations, hospitality/restaurant associations, local chambers and genuine partner/vendor sites.
- Avoid paid spammy link networks or bulk low-quality directories.

## Measurement
Track monthly: organic clicks, impressions, top queries, WhatsApp clicks, phone clicks, quote requests, GBP calls, GBP website actions and leads by service/location page.
