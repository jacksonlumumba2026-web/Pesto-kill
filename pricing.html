Detailed service guide

Clearer pest-control pricing for Nairobi customers

A price should tell a customer what they are paying for. Pestokil's published residential starting prices are a guide; the final quotation should reflect the pest, property, infestation level, treatment scope and any follow-up requirements.

Residential starting prices

Starting prices are most useful when paired with clear inclusions and exclusions. Before booking, confirm the property size, pest covered, rooms included, preparation requirements and whether follow-up is included.

Commercial quotations

Commercial work is better priced from a defined scope. Ask for the areas covered, service frequency if recurring, reporting requirements and any conditions affecting the quotation.

Why prices vary

Pest type, infestation level, number of rooms, access, treatment method, property size and follow-up requirements can all change the final cost.

Avoid surprises

If a service has a warranty or follow-up period, it should be stated in the quotation with applicable conditions. If no warranty applies, the quotation should not imply one.

Before you book

Questions customers should ask

What information should I send for a quote?

Send your Nairobi area, pest problem, property type, affected rooms and a photo if useful. The team can then confirm the next step.

Will every property receive the same treatment?

No. Treatment scope should depend on the pest, property conditions, infestation level and treatment method selected.

What should I confirm before treatment?

Confirm the areas covered, preparation, safety or re-entry guidance and any follow-up or warranty terms that apply to the quoted service.
