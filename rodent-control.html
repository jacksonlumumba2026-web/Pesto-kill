Warning Signs

Signs You Have Rats or Mice

Rodents are nocturnal and cautious — most people hear or find evidence of them long before spotting one directly.

01

Droppings

Small dark pellets near food storage, under sinks, or along skirting boards are the clearest sign of activity.

02

Scratching in Walls or Ceiling

Rodents move at night — scratching or scurrying sounds after dark usually mean they're nesting nearby.

03

Gnawed Packaging or Wires

Chewed food packets, cardboard, or electrical wiring point to an active, ongoing infestation.
