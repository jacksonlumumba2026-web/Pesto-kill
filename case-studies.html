Detailed service guide

Real Pestokil case studies should show the work, not just the promise

A useful case study explains what happened on a real job without exposing customer privacy. It should connect the problem, assessment, treatment scope, preparation and follow-up to evidence from the service record.

What every case study should contain

Property type, general Nairobi location, pest problem, visible signs, assessment findings, agreed treatment, preparation, service date and follow-up information where applicable.

Use real photographs

Before-and-after images should come from the actual job and should only be published with appropriate customer permission. Avoid stock photos presented as customer work.

Protect customer privacy

Do not publish exact residential addresses, phone numbers, private documents or identifiable customer information without appropriate consent.

Show measurable outcomes carefully

If a customer reports reduced activity or a follow-up inspection confirms an outcome, say exactly what was observed. Do not turn one job into a blanket guarantee for every property.

Before you book

Questions customers should ask

What information should I send for a quote?

Send your Nairobi area, pest problem, property type, affected rooms and a photo if useful. The team can then confirm the next step.

Will every property receive the same treatment?

No. Treatment scope should depend on the pest, property conditions, infestation level and treatment method selected.

What should I confirm before treatment?

Confirm the areas covered, preparation, safety or re-entry guidance and any follow-up or warranty terms that apply to the quoted service.
