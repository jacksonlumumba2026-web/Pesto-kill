PESTOKIL SERVICE STANDARDS

Clear pest-control information before treatment

Pestokil serves homes, apartments, landlords and businesses across Nairobi. We use a practical, property-specific approach: understand the pest problem, assess the environment, explain the proposed treatment and confirm the relevant safety and follow-up information.

What customers should receive

Assessment

The pest, signs of activity, property type and relevant conditions are considered before a treatment approach is recommended.

Quotation

Customers should understand the scope and price before authorising treatment. Where follow-up or guarantee terms apply, those terms should be stated in the quotation.

Safety information

Preparation, treatment precautions and re-entry guidance should be explained for the particular treatment being carried out.

Verification

Customers may ask Pestokil for current licences, certificates or other documentation applicable to the service before booking.

How to verify Pestokil independently

  1. Check the business name, phone number and location details shown on our website and business listings.
  2. Review genuine customer feedback on our current Google Business Profile rather than relying on testimonials reproduced on this site.
  3. Ask for current credentials or service documentation relevant to your specific treatment.
  4. For commercial work, request the proposed scope, reporting requirements and follow-up terms in writing.

Our principle: if a claim cannot be supported by current evidence, it should not be presented as a fact.
