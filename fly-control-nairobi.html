Know the signs

When Ants, Flies & Other Pests Become a Problem

Use the signs below as a starting point. A site inspection is the best way to confirm the cause and choose the right treatment.

01

Flies Around Waste

Heavy activity near bins, drains, kitchens or refuse areas can point to an accessible food or breeding source.

02

Repeated Indoor Activity

If flies keep appearing after cleaning, check drains, organic waste, damp corners and gaps around doors and windows.

03

Fruit & Drain Flies

Small flies around produce, sinks and drains can require a different approach from larger house flies.

FAQ

Fly Control Questions

Can you control flies in a restaurant?+
Yes. Restaurant fly control should combine treatment with sanitation, drainage and waste-management measures.
Why are flies coming back every day?+
Repeated fly activity usually means an accessible food source or breeding site remains nearby.
Do you treat fruit flies?+
Yes. We can assess small flies around kitchens, produce, sinks and drains and recommend the appropriate control approach.
