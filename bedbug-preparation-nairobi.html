How to Prepare for Bedbug Treatment in Nairobi

A practical preparation checklist for apartments, homes and furnished rentals before a professional bedbug treatment.

Practical guidance

1. Confirm which rooms and furniture will be treated and ask for the exact preparation instructions for your chosen method

Confirm which rooms and furniture will be treated and ask for the exact preparation instructions for your chosen method.

2. Wash or heat-treat washable bedding and clothing as instructed

Wash or heat-treat washable bedding and clothing as instructed. Keep cleaned items isolated so they are not reintroduced into treated areas.

3. Reduce clutter around beds, sofas and other harbourages so the technician can access the areas that need treatment

Reduce clutter around beds, sofas and other harbourages so the technician can access the areas that need treatment.

4. Do not move infested furniture to another room or another property unless the technician specifically advises you to do so

Do not move infested furniture to another room or another property unless the technician specifically advises you to do so.

5. After treatment, follow re-entry and follow-up instructions carefully

After treatment, follow re-entry and follow-up instructions carefully. Bedbug control may require more than one visit depending on infestation and method.
